## 2026-05-24 — Should LeWM Mastery Continue If It Is Not the Same Lane as Hardseal?

### Question

Is mastering LeWorldModel / JEPA / ARC-AGI worth pursuing if the work is not directly in the same product lane as Hardseal?

### Current answer

Yes, but with a strict boundary.

LeWM mastery is not the primary operating lane for Hardseal execution. It is a separate frontier-research lane that trains:

- representation learning;
- world-model reasoning;
- self-supervised objectives;
- evaluation discipline;
- research-code reproduction;
- first-principles mathematical thinking.

Those capabilities can strengthen long-term technical judgment, but they do not replace shipping, selling, or hardening the compliance product.

### Boundary rule

LeWM study is allowed only as a protected research block, not as an excuse to avoid product execution.

Minimum viable cadence:

- 60 minutes per day when active;
- one concrete artifact per session;
- no open-ended consumption;
- no paper is counted as read without predict, derive, log, and teach-back.

### Decision

Continue LeWM mastery as a separate research track.

Do not pretend it is the same lane as the company product.

Do not quit it because it is not the same lane.
